PII Redactor

Remove names, companies, addresses and IDs from text before you share it.

On this device. Nothing is uploaded.

1How to find it

Before you use the cloud scan

  • Your text leaves this device. It goes over HTTPS to your Modal server and is processed on a rented GPU. Nothing is stored or logged by our code, but Modal (and its cloud providers) handle it while it runs.
  • It is billed to your Modal account. Roughly 5 to 15 cents for a one-off scan (mostly the GPU starting and staying ready for 2 minutes), about 1 cent for more scans while it is warm. The first scan after a break waits 1 to 3 minutes for the GPU to start.
  • Nothing is sent automatically. A scan only runs when you press Cloud scan, and you are asked to confirm.

Set-up takes about 10 minutes: cloud/README.md. The page only connects to *.modal.run.
